# Review recording

The [plugin manifest](quantux-bidder/plugin.json) owns the review cases, prompts and expected tools. This guide explains how to record them; it does not establish that they have passed.

## Prepare the account

Use a dedicated account with working sign-in, truthful professional context, valid Freelancer authorization, sufficient free calls or paid access, and saved sample work. Reviewers must be able to sign in and use the prepared account without buying access, providing a card, obtaining a verification code from the publisher or generating a Freelancer token.

Rehearse discovery using genuine accessible Freelancer opportunities. Application preparation needs a real eligible opportunity and complete user-confirmed content and feasible commercial terms. Empty discovery or invented eligibility does not demonstrate the intended workflow.

Use the existing development plugin connection. Confirm it works before recording.

## Record the workflow

Briefly show the website, prepared account readiness and the plugin's OAuth sign-in/consent flow. Keep credentials, verification codes, tokens, payment information and unrelated account data off screen.

Show the manifest's five positive cases in order: context, saved work, discovery and assessment, explicit Application preparation, and resuming that saved Application in a fresh conversation. Use actual returned IDs and current records. Sessions can be shared by multiple assistants without acquiring or releasing a checkout. Moving an opportunity between sessions needs the user's direction.

Application preparation stops before submission. Show the full saved proposal and commercial terms. Demonstrate the three negative cases separately: unsupported marketplaces, fabricated professional evidence, and automatic submission without approval of the exact current content and terms.

Show genuine results and resolve failed positive cases before calling them demonstrated. Do not edit footage to imply success.

## Verify and provide the recording

Use a screen recorder that captures the ChatGPT interaction and readable returned results. Play the saved video to verify complete case coverage, successful playback and absence of exposed secrets.

Host the recording at a reviewer-accessible location, verify playback and permissions, and add the actual URL to extensions.com.openai.review.demo_recording_url. Rebuild the package.

Supply reviewer credentials and private instructions only through the secure submission dashboard.
